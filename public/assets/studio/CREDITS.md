# Studio assets

The office composition, custom electronics, curved stationery, paper artwork and Blender/Three.js integration were authored for Anotida Dzinotyiwei's portfolio. Project screens, the supplied CV and personal photographs are existing portfolio material.

- **Smoked Walnut Veneer**, Jenelle van Heerden, Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/smoked_walnut_veneer — CC0 1.0. Used as the desktop material; resized/compressed and tinted for the studio palette.
- **Desk Lamp Arm 01**, Kuutti Siitonen (modelling/texturing) and Yann Kervran (rigging), Poly Haven: https://polyhaven.com/a/desk_lamp_arm_01 — CC0 1.0. Rescaled, positioned and exported with compressed textures.

Poly Haven licence: https://polyhaven.com/license

Blender 4.5.9 LTS was downloaded from the Blender Foundation's official distribution and checksum-verified. It is an offline authoring tool, is not bundled with the website, and is not required by visitors or the normal website build.
